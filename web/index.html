Rev Sweeper Revolved is a fixed-screen, all-range shooter. Aim precisely, command an orbital weapon, and push through dense enemy waves under bullet-time pressure.

How to play

Game modes

Arcade is a finite run of intensifying waves and bosses; defeat the final boss to clear it. Endless continues at the selected difficulty until all lives are lost.

Orbital Weapon

Press once to deploy it around the ship, again to launch it, and once more to recall it. It also returns when its energy runs out.

Scoring

Kills raise Tension, which multiplies the value of the next kills and slowly falls over time. Score milestones award extra lives.
